DOCUMENT TITLE: Aspirin Drug Profile

Drug Name: Aspirin

Active Ingredient:
Aspirin (acetylsalicylic acid)

Drug Class:
Nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID)
Antiplatelet agent

Indications:
Aspirin is used for pain relief and fever reduction.
Low-dose aspirin is also used to reduce the risk of cardiovascular events in appropriate patients.

Mechanism of Action:
Aspirin inhibits cyclooxygenase enzymes, primarily COX-1 and COX-2.
This reduces the production of prostaglandins and thromboxanes.
Inhibition of platelet thromboxane A2 production reduces platelet aggregation.

Molecular Targets:
COX-1 (PTGS1)
COX-2 (PTGS2)

Clinical Use:
Aspirin has been studied and used in cardiovascular disease, including prevention of myocardial infarction in appropriate patient populations.

Adverse Events:
Common or clinically important adverse effects include gastrointestinal irritation and gastrointestinal bleeding.
Hypersensitivity reactions can also occur.

Important Safety Information:
The risk of gastrointestinal bleeding can increase with higher doses and with certain concomitant medications.
