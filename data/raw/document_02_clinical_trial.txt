DOCUMENT TITLE: Aspirin Clinical Study

Study Name:
Low-Dose Aspirin Clinical Study

Study Type:
Randomized clinical trial

Intervention:
Aspirin

Disease / Condition:
Colorectal adenoma

Study Population:
Adults with a history of colorectal adenomas.

Study Design:
Participants were assigned to aspirin treatment groups or placebo.

Treatment:
Participants received a low-dose aspirin regimen during the study period.

Biomarker:
Urinary PGE-M was measured as a biomarker related to prostaglandin E2 biosynthesis.

Study Objective:
The study evaluated whether low-dose aspirin affected prostaglandin-related biomarkers among individuals with colorectal adenomas.

Results:
The study compared changes in urinary PGE-M between participants receiving aspirin and participants receiving placebo.

Safety:
No serious adverse events were reported during the study.
Minor adverse events and participant complaints occurred at similar frequencies across treatment groups.

Conclusion:
The study provided clinical evidence regarding the biological effects and safety of low-dose aspirin in individuals with colorectal adenomas.
